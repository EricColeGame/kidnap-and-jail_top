## Beginner Capturing Tactics: How to Bag and Secure Targets

Bagging another player requires more than simply running up and swinging your capture tool. Experienced targets constantly monitor their camera angles and stay near obstacles, meaning careless approaches will get you counter-attacked or out-sprinted.

To successfully pull off a capture as detailed in this **kidnap and jail beginner guide**, focus on blind spots, corners, and distracted players. Look for targets who are managing menus, browsing shops, or looting resources. Once you close the distance, deploying your restraint gear quickly is vital before your target can initiate a sprint boost.

```
[Target Spotted] ➔ [Check Flanks / Sightlines] ➔ [Deploy Stun / Taser] ➔ [Apply Bag / Restraint] ➔ [Carry to Transport] ➔ [Lock in Cell]
```

Once a player is restrained, the danger does not vanish. In fact, carrying a captive slows your base movement speed, making you an enticing target for third-party ambushers or the captive’s allies.

| Equipment Tier | Capture Speed | Durability / Escape Time | Movement Penalty While Carrying | Best Beginner Scenario |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Canvas Sack** | 2.5 seconds | Low (Target escapes in ~6s) | -25% Speed | Solo isolated targets near cell drop-offs |
| **Reinforced Burlap Bag** | 1.8 seconds | Medium (Target escapes in ~10s) | -20% Speed | General urban captures and bounty runs |
| **Heavy-Duty Zip Straps** | 1.2 seconds | High (Target escapes in ~15s) | -15% Speed | Fast-paced combat captures |
| **Armored Containment Rig**| 0.8 seconds | Maximum (Requires ally lockpick) | -10% Speed | Squad-based operations and high-bounty targets |

Always park your transport vehicle nearby before initiating an ambush. Carrying a struggling captive through broad daylight for hundreds of meters invites disaster.

## Evasion and Self-Defense: Staying Out of the Cell

Avoiding capture requires proactive map awareness rather than purely reactive dodging. Once a captor attaches a bag or restraint tool, your escape options narrow quickly to button-mashing or relying on a nearby teammate.

Following the evasion principles of our **kidnap and jail beginner guide**, your best defense is maintaining spatial distance. Never linger in choke points, narrow hallways, or isolated blind alleys without an exit route planned. When entering buildings or shopping hubs, position your avatar near multiple exits.

### Crucial Defensive Habits
* **Camera Orbiting:** Continuously rotate your camera 360 degrees when looting or standing still.
* **Sound Awareness:** Listen for sprinting footsteps, bag rustling, or engine revs that signal an approaching kidnapper.
* **Stamina Conservation:** Never drain your stamina meter completely; reserve at least 30% for emergency sprints when ambushed.
* **Defensive Item Slots:** Keep a defensive tool like a flash grenade, smoke bomb, or stun gadget bound to an easily accessible hotkey.

If a captor does manage to tackle you, immediately begin the breakout mini-game while calling out your location to friends or allies. The faster you break the initial grip, the less time they have to drag you to a lockup facility.

## Prison Breakout Guide: Escaping Confinement Safely

Sooner or later, every beginner ends up behind bars. Being jailed is not a game-over screen; it simply shifts your gameplay loop into an escape puzzle. The jail complex features multiple security layers, including guarded corridors, locked iron gates, ventilation ducts, and perimeter fences.

In this **kidnap and jail beginner guide**, we recommend familiarizing yourself with the prison blueprint before attempting a breakout. Rushing the front doors almost always results in getting tased, re-arrested, and slapped with longer jail timers.

## Advanced Survival and Capture Tips

Once you have mastered the basics of this **kidnap and jail beginner guide**, incorporating nuanced tactical tricks will give you a decisive edge during chaotic public lobby matches.

### Utilize Decoys and Fake Escapes
When escaping from prison, opening multiple cell doors or causing a commotion in the yard draws guard attention away from the sewer exits. If playing as a hunter, leave an empty vehicle with its lights on near a busy avenue; curious players often stop to inspect it, creating a prime ambush opportunity.

### Manage Your Weight and Inventory
Carrying multiple heavy tools can decrease your agility in certain game configurations. Keep your inventory tidy, carrying only your primary restraint tool, one escape item, and a mobility booster.

### Teamwork and Role Synergy
Playing with a partner doubles your survivability. One player acts as the "Bait" to lure overly aggressive kidnappers into an alleyway, while the hidden "Striker" ambushes the pursuer with a stun tool from behind.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do immediately after entering a server?
As outlined in this kidnap and jail beginner guide, your first move should be scouting the safe zones and locating the prison exits. Acquire basic footwear or stamina perks before attempting to hunt other players so you can outrun early ambushes.

### How do I break out of a capture bag without tools?
If you are bagged without items, you must quickly tap or click the on-screen struggle prompts. While this will not instantly free you from high-tier restraints, community reports confirm that persistent struggling severely penalizes the captor's movement speed and can allow you to break free if they get obstructed by terrain.

### Can allies free me from the back of a captor's vehicle?
Yes. If your teammates intercept the captor's vehicle, disabling the vehicle or engaging the driver forces them out. Allies can interact with the vehicle's rear compartment using lockpicks or crowbars to break you out before you reach the jail processing desk.

### What is the fastest way to earn money as a beginner?
The most reliable method is capturing distracted, low-bounty targets located near the jail perimeter. This minimizes travel time, reduces the risk of ambushes, and guarantees steady payouts so you can afford essential gear upgrades quickly.
