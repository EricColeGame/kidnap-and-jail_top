## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best keybindings for kidnap and jail PC to carry opponents quickly?
Community players typically recommend keeping movement bound to standard `WASD` while remapping interact (`E`) and carry/shoulder actions (`R`) to your mouse side buttons (`Mouse 4` and `Mouse 5`). This enables you to grab downed targets, initiate restraints, and run without ever lifting your fingers off your movement keys.

### Why is my breakout struggle meter filling slowly on desktop?
Slow struggle rates on PC usually stem from keyboard ghosting or improper input cadence. Instead of mashing multiple keys at the exact same moment, alternate between your assigned struggle keys (such as `A` and `D`) with distinct, rhythmic presses. Additionally, make sure background applications are not capturing global keystrokes.

### Can I play kidnap and jail PC using an Xbox or PlayStation controller?
Yes, most PC gaming clients support plug-and-play gamepads alongside mouse and keyboard. However, while controllers offer analog movement, competitive community reports suggest that mouse aiming provides superior precision for landing subdual strikes and executing rapid lockpicking minigames.

### How do I fix camera spin or cursor unlocking issues in kidnap and jail PC?
If your camera spins out of control or your mouse cursor drifts off the screen during gameplay, toggle your Shift Lock switch in the platform settings menu. Pressing `Right Shift` or your designated camera lock key locks the mouse cursor directly into the center of the viewport, eliminating multi-monitor cursor escape and drift.
