## Core Default Keybinds: PC, Mobile, and Console Breakdown

Every platform delivers a distinct tactile experience. On PC, keyboard agility allows for immediate hotbar toggling and micro-adjustments during chases. On consoles, analog sticks grant superior steering and directional fluidness, while mobile layouts condense interaction prompts into intuitive touch zones.

Reviewing your platform's base layout prevents costly mistakes when shifting roles from innocent civilian to aggressive warden or cunning kidnapper.

## Captor Mechanics: How to Kidnap, Transport, and Lock Up Rivals

Playing as an aggressor or correctional officer requires chaining multiple inputs together. You cannot simply walk up to a standing opponent and lock them behind bars. You must first incapacitate or corner them, secure your restraints, transport them safely, and finalize the sentence in an official cell block.

Executing fluent inputs under pressure separates elite captors from novice players who let their bounties slip away.

```
[Incapacitate Target] ➔ [Equip Restraints (1-9)] ➔ [Hold Interact (E / X / Tap)] ➔ [Carry / Vehicle Load] ➔ [Deposit in Jail Cell]
```

### Step-by-Step Restraint and Transfer Sequencing

Capturing opponents requires strict adherence to proximity windows and timing constraints. If you activate your carry tool too early, the target can dodge and counter-attack.

| Phase | Required Keybind / Action | Mechanism Details | Pro Failure State to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Neutralization** | Primary Strike (`LMB` / `RT`) | Deplete target stamina or land a stun attack | Attacking while out of range, wasting stamina |
| **2. Restraint Application** | Hotbar `1`–`3`, then Hold `E` / `Square` | Fasten zip-ties, ropes, or cuffs within 2 meters | Releasing the key early before the meter fills |
| **3. Hoisting / Carrying** | Tap `E` / Context Action | Lift restrained player onto your shoulder | Moving backward while hoisting, causing drop desync |
| **4. Vehicular Transport** | Interact with Door (`E` / `X`) | Place prisoner in car trunk or backseat | Getting hijacked by the prisoner's allies |
| **5. Cell Containment** | Proximity Prompt at Cell Gate | Slide prisoner past the threshold and lock door | Leaving cell open while managing inventory |

Player experience shared across community forums emphasizes maintaining forward momentum while lifting. If you stop moving while initiating the hoist animation, you become an easy target for enemy snipers or ambushers trying to disrupt the arrest.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Kidnap and Jail Controls

### What are the default kidnap and jail controls for capturing a player?
On PC, incapacitate your target, select your restraint tool using number keys `1` through `9`, and hold `E` while standing next to the victim. Once cuffed, tap `E` again to hoist them onto your shoulder. To drop or jail them, navigate to a cell or vehicle and press `Q` or the contextual interact button. On console, use `X` (Xbox) or `Square` (PlayStation) to carry out the same sequence.

### Why do my kidnap and jail controls feel delayed when I try to arrest someone?
Input lag in capture games is usually caused by server tickrate latency, high client ping, or animation locking. If the target is moving rapidly, their server position might be slightly behind where you see them on your screen. Lead your target by half a step before initiating the grab prompt, and ensure your chat box or inventory menus are closed so your interaction keys register instantly.

### Can I remap my kidnap and jail controls on mobile devices?
Most mobile sandbox titles feature fixed UI layouts, but many allow you to adjust button size, button opacity, and virtual joystick sensitivity via the in-game settings menu. Increasing the touch radius for the interact and sprint buttons prevents accidental misclicks when scrambling to arrest opponents or break out of jail.

### How do I break out of handcuffs using the escape controls?
When restrained, a struggle widget appears on your screen. On PC, rapidly alternate pressing the `A` and `D` keys or tap `Spacebar` depending on the specific minigame mechanic. On controllers, waggle your Left Analog stick left and right or rapidly tap `A`/`Cross`. Filling the breakout meter before your captor reaches a holding cell drops you safely to your feet, allowing you to sprint away.
