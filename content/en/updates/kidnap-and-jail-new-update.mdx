## Reworked Kidnapping and Capture Mechanics

Capturing an opposing player is no longer a simple button-press affair. With the arrival of this **kidnap and jail new update**, attackers must manage restraint durability, victim stamina, and transportation logistics simultaneously.

### The Restraint and Escort System

When an abductor incapacitates an opponent, applying restraints initiates an active contest. The victim receives a rhythm-based struggle prompt, while the captor must maintain close physical proximity to tighten binds. If an escorting player attempts to sprint across open terrain, the captive's struggle bar fills significantly faster, creating opportunities for sudden breaks.

Furthermore, carrying a captive imposes an explicit carry-weight debuff:
* **Movement Speed:** Decreased by 18% during standard escort.
* **Jump Height:** Capped to baseline clearance, preventing acrobatic ledge escapes while hauling players.
* **Weapon Handling:** Limited exclusively to sidearms or single-handed deterrents.

## Defense, Counter-Play, and Rescue Strategies

Surviving against coordinated kidnapping squads requires situational awareness and proactive defense. The **kidnap and jail new update** introduces several mechanics that enable potential victims to defend themselves before capture sequences initiate.

### Awareness and Anti-Ambush Tactics

* **Audio Cues:** Heavy restraint equipment emits metallic jingling sounds within a 15-meter radius, alerting attentive players to flanking abductors.
* **Distress Flare Beacons:** When pinned down, captives have a two-second window before full immobilization to drop a flare, pinging their immediate coordinates to allied faction members.
* **Defensive Decoys:** Deployable holographic or stationary dummies distract automated targeting tools and net traps, buying valuable seconds to retaliate.

### Lockpicking and Jailbreak Execution

If an arrest or kidnapping succeeds, confinement does not mean defeat. Tool concealment is a core skill introduced in the latest mechanics. Inmates can conceal miniature lockpicks in boot linings, avoiding detection during standard check-in frisks.

```
Acquire Contraband Pick ──> Evade Guard Frisk ──> Pick Cell Tumblers ──> Disable Wing Power ──> Perimeter Sprint
```

When picking locks, players solve a circular alignment puzzle. Misaligning pins alerts nearby security units, making calm execution mandatory under pressure.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most significant change in the kidnap and jail new update?
The most impactful addition is the interactive struggle and restraint resistance system. Instead of remaining completely immobilized upon capture, victims can complete skill checks to break free or signal allies, while captors must actively manage transport speeds and restraint integrity.

### How does the new security tier system determine prison placement?
Your placement depends on your active bounty bracket and escape track record. Low-threat offenders spawn in minimum-security cells with accessible maintenance ducts, whereas high-notoriety fugitives go directly to fortified maximum-security vaults equipped with automated turrets and electronic lock systems.

### Can kidnappers still camp safe zones or respawn points?
No. The latest balance pass introduces a strict 15-second capture immunity window for recently spawned players in public districts. Additionally, entering designated non-combat zones instantly releases any active escort tethers, preventing players from being dragged into protected territory.

### How do lockpicks work after the kidnap and jail new update?
Lockpicks now require completing an interactive tumbler puzzle rather than functioning as an instant-use consumable. Successfully aligning the pins quietly pops cell gates, but failing the alignment produces a distinct audio ping that alerts nearby guards and wardens to your breakout attempt.
